# Hook the Happyness — Fishing Pond (English Introduction)

*Source: hookfishpond.com · drafted 2026-10-11*

---

## Take Home the Fish You Catch — That's the Whole Idea

Hook the Happyness is a Thai fishing pond where the best part of the trip is the one you carry out the door: **the fish you catch are yours to keep**. Bring your own rod for 100 baht a day, or rent one from our wall for 150 baht, cast your line, and when you're done, every fish that comes up the water belongs on your table — no buying it back, no "you can't take it out" surprise. That single rule is what makes this a different kind of fishing day, and it's the reason families, friends, and serious anglers all keep coming back.

We built this pond from bare ground in just six months, photographed every step, and opened the kitchen and rod wall this year. What you'll find today is a place that feels like it's been here for decades, even though it's brand new.

## Two Ponds, Two Kinds of Day

**The Leisure Pond** is where the relaxed, hands-on-water-with-the-fish kind of visit happens. Around 30 fishing spots, open all day from 09:00 to 18:00 (we're closed Mondays). No time-slot games, no peak-rate math — whatever hour you arrive, the price is the same. Bring the kids, book an afternoon, let the little ones tag along and watch them reel in their first fish. It's the kind of day that ends with dinner from our kitchen and a photo with the catch.

**The Competition Pond** is for the people who came here to actually fish hard. It's 500 baht per person per day, groups of 10 to 40, and the whole day belongs to your team. We weigh every fish and rank you by total weight; the winner takes a surprise prize we announce on the spot. Here all catch is released back in, so the pond stays stocked and the challenge stays fair.

## What You Can Catch — and Take

Both ponds are stocked with the fish Thai people actually cook: Nile tilapia, snakehead, climbing perch, silver barb, marbled goby, walking catfish, and striped catfish. You're welcome to bring any of them home. The one exception is the **Mekong Giant Catfish (pla buak)** — a real trophy, and it goes back into the water regardless of size, so the big ones keep the pond exciting for everyone.

Our catch-home policy is the most generous we've seen in the region: **the first kilo is free**, then 60 baht per kilogram, weighed right at the counter in front of you, no argument, no second-guessing. If you'd rather not take your fish home, our kitchen will process it for you — steamed, fried, or grilled, 100 baht per fish, a cheaper and cleaner deal than you'd find at any Japanese-style fishing park we benchmarked against.

## More Than a Pond — It's a Whole Afternoon

The pond is the anchor, but the day gets better once you're in. Thatched pavilions and a fishing walkway frame the water, night lights come on after dark (and honestly, the pond looks better under the lights than in the sun), and there's a proper dining area where our menu runs grilled whole fish, skewers, Thai staples, and cold drinks. Scan the QR on your table, order, and pay with PromptPay when you're ready — no cash juggling, no "where's the bill?" chase.

We also run a small rewards program that's fun enough to mention on its own: land a big one and we hand you a badge and a discount for your next visit; keep releasing your trophies and you earn stamps that turn into a free session. It's a nice reason to come back, and most visitors tell us it's the thing that pulls them the second time.

## Practical Details (the kind you actually want to know)

- **Where:** hookfishpond.com, book online in three languages — Chinese, English, and Thai.
- **When:** 09:00–18:00, every day except Mondays.
- **Entry:** 100 baht per rod (own rod) or 150 baht per rod (rented) — pick one, no stacking.
- **Competition groups:** 500 baht per person per day, 10–40 anglers.
- **Rules that matter:** no outside bait (we supply it all), no outside food-and-drink (a few exceptions for baby food and medication), and no swimming in the pond. Safety first, always.
- **If something breaks:** there's no deposit, and damage to gear is a flat 1,000 baht. Simple, transparent, settled at the counter when you hand back your rod.

## Why You'll Come Back

The first visit is about the novelty — "wait, I actually get to keep what I caught?" The second visit is about everything else: the night lights, the food, the little kid who got his first-catch certificate and a sticker, the big-fish badge that earns you 50 baht off next time, the surprise prize at a competition you might have won.

That's the hook (pun intended) we built this place for. One day at the water, dinner on the table, and a reason to be back next weekend.

**Hook the Happyness — take your catch home.**
