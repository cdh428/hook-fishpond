## 中文

**📌 价格与服务调整公告**

**乐钓湾钓鱼场（HOOK THE HAPPYNESS）**

服务时间：09:00 – 18:00　每周一休息

因当前洪水灾情及鱼类成本上涨，本店自 **2026 年 10 月 16 日** 起调整价格：

- 自带竿费：每竿 100 泰铢（含 1 公斤免费鱼）

## English

**📌 PRICE AND SERVICE ADJUSTMENT ANNOUNCEMENT**

**HOOK THE HAPPYNESS FISHING POND**

SERVICE HOURS: 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM · CLOSED EVERY MONDAY

Due to the current flood situation and rising fish costs, we would like to inform you of our new price schedule, effective from **October 16, 2026**:

- Own-rod fee: 100 Baht per rod (includes 1 kg of free fish)
- Rod rental fee: 150 Baht per rod (includes 1 kg of free fish)
- Additional fish can be purchased for take-home if desired
- Please use bait from the pond only

**(TAKE-HOME FISH PRICES)**
- Striped Catfish — 50 Baht/kg
- Walking Catfish / Silver Barb / Climbing Perch — 60 Baht/kg
- Tilapia / Snakehead — 80 Baht/kg
- Marbled Goby — 100 Baht/kg

⚠️ Fish weighing over 4 kilograms (per fish) are considered game fish and are strictly prohibited from being taken home!
🚫 Large Mekong Giant Catfish (Pla Buek) must not be removed 🚫
